CHAPTER 01

The Worst Day Ever

“NOOOOO! THAT'S IMPOSSIBLE!”

Eleven-year-old Zayn slammed his controller onto the sofa. GAME OVER. Again. His fifth defeat in a row.

“This game is stupid!” he shouted. His little brother Hamza peeked into the room. “Maybe you're just bad at it.”

“GET OUT!” yelled Zayn. Hamza ran off laughing.

Just then Mum called, “Zayn! Have you finished your homework?” Zayn froze. Homework?! He'd completely forgotten.

He stormed to his room, but something mysterious waited on his desk: a small black box glowing bright blue.

⚡ THE PAUSE DEVICE — VERSION 1.0
WARNING: This device can change your life.

“Finally. Something interesting!” Zayn pressed the button.

BZZZZZZT! The lights went out, and a voice whispered… “Welcome, Player One.”

CHAPTER 02

The Secret Power

A tiny blue holographic robot floated above the box. “Hello, Zayn. I'm PIX.”

“WHAT?! A robot?!”

“Obviously,” PIX replied. “And you have a problem.” A floating report appeared in the air.

🎮 PLAYER REPORT
😡 Angry reactions: VERY HIGH
👉 Blaming others: FREQUENT
🧠 Thinking before acting: NEEDS UPGRADE

“HEY! Who gave you permission to judge me?”

PIX folded his metal arms. “Interesting. Another angry reaction.”

“Fine! What does your silly gadget do?” Zayn asked.

“It gives you something better than unlimited diamonds or infinite health.”

“Impossible!”

“The power to choose what you do next.”

“Sounds boring,” said Zayn.

PIX smiled. “Then let's play a game.”

CHAPTER 03

The Three-Second Challenge

The next morning at school, Zayn reached into his bag for his science project. Nothing. His heart sank. He'd left it at home!

“This is all Mum's fault! She rushed me,” he muttered.

Then his watch buzzed. BEEP! BEEP! A tiny PIX appeared.

🎯 MISSION 1 · THE MISSING PROJECT

You're Zayn. What would you do?

Choose an answer to unlock your mission points.

Zayn took a deep breath. “Miss, I finished my project, but I left it at home. I'm sorry. May I bring it tomorrow?”

The teacher nodded. “Thank you for being honest. Bring it first thing tomorrow.”

🏆 MISSION COMPLETE! · +100 XP in the story

“You couldn't change what happened,” PIX said, “but you could choose what to do next.”

Zayn smiled. “Okay… that was actually pretty cool.”

CHAPTER 04

The Anger Monster

At lunchtime, the football match was tied 2–2. Rayyan passed. Zayn kicked. A defender blocked the ball… and the other team scored! 3–2. GAME OVER!

“YOU RUINED EVERYTHING!” Zayn shouted at Rayyan.

Suddenly, the sky turned red. A giant smoky monster appeared. “I AM THE ANGER MONSTER! SHOUT LOUDER!”

“PIX! WHAT IS THAT?!”

“Your anger is trying to take charge,” PIX replied. “You have three seconds!”

3… 2… 1… PAUSE! ⏸

Zayn breathed. “I'm disappointed we lost, Rayyan. But it wasn't all your fault. I missed the shot too.”

POOOOF! The monster vanished into smoke.

🎯 MISSION 2 · THE TEAMMATE TEST

Your teammate makes a mistake in a game. What's the hero move?

Choose a move.

Rayyan grinned. “Rematch tomorrow?” “Definitely,” Zayn replied.

“Remember,” said PIX, “being angry is okay. But anger doesn't get to make your choices.”

CHAPTER 05 · FINAL BOSS

The Controller Was You

That evening, Zayn saw Hamza sitting on the floor holding his favourite controller. It was broken.

“WHAT. DID. YOU. DO?” Hamza's eyes filled with tears. “Sorry… I dropped it.”

The lights flickered. The Anger Monster returned, ten times bigger!

“HE BROKE YOUR CONTROLLER! MAKE HIM CRY!” the monster roared.

PIX whispered, “You can't undo the accident. But you can choose what happens next.”

Zayn breathed slowly. “I'm really upset. That controller matters to me. Please ask before using my things next time.”

Hamza nodded. “Can we try to fix it?”

“Yeah,” Zayn said, “let's see what's broken.”

KABOOOOOOM! The monster exploded into golden sparks.

🏆 FINAL BOSS DEFEATED!
✨ NEW SKILL: SELF-CONTROL
🔓 HABIT #1 UNLOCKED: BE PROACTIVE

“You never needed a magical device,” PIX said. “You already had the power to PAUSE, THINK, and CHOOSE.”

On Zayn's watch, one final message appeared:

🎮 THE REAL CONTROLLER WAS YOU ALL ALONG.

Zayn couldn't control everything that happened around him. But he could control how he responded.
